## When and where

**Saturday, November 14, 2026, 11:45 am to 12:45 pm**, at the 93rd Annual Meeting of the APS Southeastern Section (SESAPS), KSU Center, 3333 Busbee Drive NW, Kennesaw, GA 30144. It is the last session of the meeting and may run ten minutes over.

A moderated discussion, not a panel: contributors and attendees work through the questions the one-page contributions raise, with a written summary and a paper to follow. Registration for the meeting is required (there is no single-day rate); the organizers have travel awards for session participants. Details and fees: [SESAPS 2026 at Kennesaw State](https://www.kennesaw.edu/conferences/sesaps-conference.php).
