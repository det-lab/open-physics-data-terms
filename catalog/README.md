`catalog.csv` is the human-readable summary table shown on the website.
The full coded sheet (every codebook field, one row per release) lives in
the project's Google Sheet until the coding is finished, then is exported
here as `catalog-full.csv`.

`status` is `pilot` (October 2026 pilot, AI first pass, human check in
progress), `checked` (a person has verified every cell), or `contributor`
(the collaboration itself confirmed the row).
